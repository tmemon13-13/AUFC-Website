Our Story

About AUFC

How a vision for something different became one of Northern Virginia's most exciting soccer clubs.

The Beginning

A Club Built on Belief

Alexandria United FC was born from a conviction that Northern Virginia — one of the most diverse, passionate soccer communities in the country — deserved a club that felt like its own. Not a franchise. Not a product. A club.

Founded by Hatzael Diaz, AUFC brought together players, coaches, and community members who shared a single goal: to build something authentic in Alexandria. Something that would represent not just a city, but a culture.

From our first match under the floodlights at local fields, to organized league competition, Alexandria United FC has grown into more than a team. We're a movement — one fueled by the belief that the beautiful game makes every community stronger.

Our Mission

To Unite Alexandria Through Soccer

We exist to create a competitive, community-rooted soccer program that uplifts players, engages local businesses, and makes Alexandria proud. On and off the pitch, every decision we make comes back to people.

Our Vision

A Club Alexandria Can Call Its Own

We envision AUFC as a lasting institution in the DMV — one that develops players, builds community, attracts top-tier sponsorship, and represents Alexandria on the regional and national stage with excellence and integrity.

What We Stand For

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do — from training to the final whistle.

⚽

Excellence

We compete to win and we train to be our best. Excellence on the pitch means showing up with full effort, discipline, and a hunger to improve — every single session.

🤝

Community

AUFC belongs to Alexandria. We invest in the neighborhoods we represent, partner with local businesses, and believe that soccer is a force for connection and belonging.

🌍

Inclusivity

What Sets Us Apart
